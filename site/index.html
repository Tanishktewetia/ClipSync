Does it work without the internet?

Clipboard traffic goes directly over your local network. Your devices need to be on the same reachable Wi-Fi or PC hotspot; internet is only needed to download the apps or open online help.

Why isn't sending from Android automatic?

Android restricts background clipboard reads. Tap Send clipboard now in the notification, or Send to PC in Quick Settings, to deliberately share your copied text. No extra keyboard or helper app is needed.

What happens when I lock my phone?

ClipSync holds only the latest incoming PC text in memory, then applies it when you unlock. It does not keep a clipboard history on disk. Android may still show its system clipboard indicator.

Can I share images or use an iPhone?

Not in this beta. ClipSync currently supports plain text between Windows and Android. Images, iOS, and remote internet syncing are not included.

Is this ready for a public production rollout?

This is an early, debug-signed beta for testing. Review the source, verify the download checksum, and follow the setup guide. There is no automatic updater or Play Store release.
